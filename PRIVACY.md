# Privacy Policy - 10 Second Rule

10 Second Rule stores its settings, grace timestamps, and per-site counters (times the gate was shown and completed) in `chrome.storage.local` on your device.

- No data is sent anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.
- No analytics, tracking, accounts, or remote code.
- The extension does not read or store page content or browsing history. It only compares the current site's hostname to your list.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data.
